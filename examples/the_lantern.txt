A traveler reaches a quiet village carrying a lantern that no longer gives light. The villagers warn that the old road is closed.

At dusk, the lantern glows beside a wall and reveals a path into the forest. The traveler follows it and finds a child waiting beside a broken bridge.

The bridge can be repaired only by giving up the map that leads home. The traveler must choose between returning safely and opening a way for the child.

The traveler burns the map, crosses at dawn, and carries the lantern back to the village. It shines again when the villagers decide to keep the road open for everyone.
